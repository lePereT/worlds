# Restrictive Theory follow-up

Status: research only. No production Worlds source is changed.

This lab follows two results from the restrictive Theory audit:

1. explicit Theory retraction may be unnecessary if mutable knowledge has exact lifecycle support;
2. the richer Construction correspondence required by compositional knowledge transport may be the
   same provenance structure whose surviving shadow generated the native between-Worlds groupoid.

The lab is now partly historical: Worlds 0.6.6 has adopted native Construction correspondence. The lifecycle pressures remain useful, while knowledge transport now consumes the native evidence through the thin Knowledge host rather than the old lab relation API.

## Experiment A: positive Theory with exact support

The old extended-Theory no-retraction experiment now lives under `historical/`. It is deliberately
segregated from the executable thin Knowledge contract: it remains falsification evidence for the
lifecycle claim, not an alternative Theory implementation.

`historical/01_support_lifetime.lua` pressures the intended lifecycle law:

- contradictory functional knowledge about one surviving Point is rejected;
- compatible knowledge on the same surviving exact support may accumulate;
- mutable state is represented by a Strand occurrence which is consumed and replaced by a successor;
- old knowledge disappears from the public State when its exact support is no longer on the boundary;
- a previously prepared Rule whose Theory premise lost its support becomes stale even while unrelated
  exact authority survives.

`historical/run_legacy_48.lua` runs all 48 existing extended-Theory hostile pressures through a compatibility
adapter which erases their legacy retract lists before the strict positive engine sees the Rule. All
48 still pass.

This proves only that the retract channel is redundant for those pressures. The positive engine is
otherwise the old hostile-lab Theory implementation; in particular its boundary projector is not the
new exact regular-doctrine implementation.

## Experiment B: one Construction correspondence

The old research `correspondence.lua` has been deleted. The pressures call the native Worlds 0.6.6
`Construction:correspondence()` object directly. Native correspondence retains source-only quotient
classes, composes across staged Construction with fresh parts, and supplies source-relative codomain
mates directly.

The same object has two uses.

First, native Worlds correspondence transports positive knowledge through the thin host, preserving constraints created by an intermediate identity quotient even when that quotient class is subsequently annihilated. The final partial `Construction:image` alone cannot do this.

Second, native correspondence directly induces the exact source-relative endpoint map used by the
between-Worlds construction-groupoid experiments.
