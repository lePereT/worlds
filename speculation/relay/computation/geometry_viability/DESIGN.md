# Exact boundary-Development viability for recursive Judgement

## Thesis

The previous stable-recursive-Judgement laboratory deliberately returned `Deferred` when recursive
preference depended on authority Geometry. This laboratory closes that gap without introducing an effect,
capability, task or scheduler abstraction.

Stable recursive viability is the least fixed point of a finite quotient of **boundary Developments**.
Each viability case keeps Geometry and Theory paired. Search may discover any finite representative of a
case; Judgement proves eligibility from the semantic case fixed point rather than from search depth.

The recurrence Package representation is unchanged: it is still a finite cyclic presentation of open
Development contexts. Every finite realised descendant is still an ordinary relative Development.

## Why the summary is paired

Authority viability and knowledge viability cannot be analysed independently. A preferred family may have
one finite descendant whose Geometry is compatible but whose Theory is contradictory, and another whose
Theory is compatible but whose Geometry cannot close against the live state. Neither descendant is viable.
Taking the Cartesian product of independent Geometry and Theory summaries would invent a false blocker.

Therefore the atomic Judgement summary is one boundary Development case, not a pair of unrelated abstract
analyses.

## Authority boundary quotient

The Geometry component retains only exact structure which future boundary composition can observe in the
pressured fragment:

- the complete two-sided open Strand boundary;
- exact Strand multiplicity and identity within that boundary;
- ordered Point incidence, including Point sharing across Strands;
- Membrane identity and ancestry supporting those Points and Strands; and
- causal reachability from open ingress Strands to open egress Strands.

Closed interior Faces and path history are forgotten. A one-Face step, a deeper causal chain and a closed
split/merge diamond therefore collapse when they have the same external authority support and causal
relation. An identity wire remains distinct from a causal transition.

The hostile contextual pass extends that claim beyond simple wrappers. Equal boundary cases remain equal
when the enclosing Development also captures an independent scarce Strand. A split/merge shell crossing a
recursive boundary saturates after its external causal relation is established. A lawful locality excursion
through an ancestor is forgotten when the same child-locality support returns to the public boundary, while
a public change of locality remains distinct. Exact feedback may annihilate an internal authority cycle;
Construction correspondence still transports Theory across the cuts before the hidden occurrence is
existentially projected away.

This is a Judgement quotient of ordinary Geometry, not a new semantic IR. The laboratory pressure-tests
contextual congruence but does not claim a general full-abstraction proof for every future Worlds extension.

## Complete recursive authority boundary

A recurrence Development context must present the complete exact open authority boundary, one-to-one, on
its semantic recurrence interface. Otherwise an omitted open ingress could silently remain an unsatisfied
requirement, or fresh open authority could accumulate across repeated unfolding outside the supposedly
stable recursive interface.

This does not make client Presentation authoritative. Presentation may later reorder, omit or duplicate
semantic ports for human or API use. The completeness rule applies to the semantic recurrence boundary
used for Package composition and Judgement.

## Joint occurrence-relative knowledge

The Theory component is the restrictive positive knowledge relation projected to the union of:

- Points supporting the exact open authority boundary; and
- public value Points.

The projection is existential, so hidden internal witnesses disappear while any positive boundary
consequence they entail survives. A relation coupling an authority-support Point to a public value Point
remains one relation; it is not split into two analyses.

At live Judgement, state knowledge may be supplied as legacy public-value knowledge, exact occurrence
knowledge over live Worlds Points, or one joint mixed relation preserving correlations between both.

## Pulling Rule requirements to the live state

A finite candidate is freshly instantiated and joined to the selected exact live authority using ordinary
`Construction.join`.

Its positive input Theory requirement is then pulled back to the live input context:

1. public value input Points are related to canonical value coordinates;
2. authority-support Points are related to selected live Points only through native Construction quotient
   correspondence;
3. all exact identifications are imposed before existential hiding; and
4. the resulting one relation is judged against live value/occurrence knowledge.

Thus one Rule Point identified with two live Points constrains both live Points. Conversely, two Rule
Points identified by one shared live Point must carry compatible Theory knowledge. No separate alias or
capability analysis is required.

`Hit`, `Retry` and `Unknown` are derived from the restrictive knowledge information order. `Unknown` keeps
a dominating alternative viable. Positive contradiction is required to eliminate it.

## Recursive fixed point

For every recursive root and every alternative, analysis starts from the empty set of completed boundary
cases. It repeatedly substitutes already-known child-case representatives into ordinary Development
contexts using `Context.plug`, canonicalises the resulting paired boundary case, and adds genuinely new
cases until no case changes.

Distinct finite histories with the same paired boundary case collapse. Recursive chain depth therefore
does not make the semantic viability analysis grow after its boundary cases have saturated.

Ordered-family dominance is not part of the viability key. Preference is consulted only by Judgement.

The same fixed point is computed across mutually recursive semantic roots. Authority transfer between roots
is ordinary recursive plugging and exact cuts; no root-local effect state is introduced. Dominance may cross
those roots, but stable blocking is still decided only by complete paired Development cases.

## Stable blocker proof

Search returns an ephemeral finite candidate with a derivation tree. To test a dominating alternative at
any node in that derivation, Judgement substitutes each semantic boundary-case representative for the
chosen node and rebuilds the complete enclosing root candidate with ordinary `Context.plug`.

The rebuilt whole candidate is tested against the same exact live Geometry and joint occurrence knowledge.
A dominator blocks only if at least one complete finite descendant is genuinely compatible in that whole
context.

This avoids inventing a local authority-state abstraction for nested recursion. It also means the current
finite search frontier is never evidence of stable eligibility.

## Semantic statuses

- `Hit`: a discovered finite candidate's input requirements are positively established and no dominating
  finite descendant remains viable.
- `Blocked`: a dominating alternative has at least one compatible finite completion.
- `Retry`: no finite semantic case is compatible with the exact live state/knowledge.
- `Deferred`: semantic viability remains, but current discovery has not supplied a certified stable witness.
- `Unknown`: an internal Theory judgement saying a requirement is consistent but not established. It is not
  solver exhaustion.

## What the laboratory refuses to add

There is no authority effect set, capability summary, task graph, scheduler state, recursive instruction,
continuation object, mutable Theory state or Package evaluator.

The finite viability evidence is derived entirely from Development: exact boundary Geometry plus
occurrence-relative positive Theory.
