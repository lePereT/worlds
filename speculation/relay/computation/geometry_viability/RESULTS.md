# Results

## Outcome

The authority-dependent stable-Judgement gap can be closed without a second effect/capability algebra.
Stable recursive viability is represented as the least fixed point of paired boundary Development cases:
complete exact open authority Geometry plus restrictive occurrence-relative boundary Theory.

The laboratory passes 59 pressures with 581 direct assertions.

## Main findings

### Geometry and Theory must remain correlated

Independent summaries are unsound. A preferred family can contain a Geometry-compatible/Theory-impossible
descendant and a Theory-compatible/Geometry-impossible descendant. No actual descendant blocks, even
though independent existential summaries would say that both sides are viable. Paired Development cases
preserve the correlation and allow the fallback.

### The authority quotient is small in the pressured fragment

Future exact closure distinguishes open Strand identity/multiplicity, ordered Point incidence and sharing,
Membrane ancestry, and causal reachability from ingress to egress. Closed Face history does not survive.
Deep causal chains and closed split/merge diamonds collapse to the same case as a simple causal step when
those boundary observables agree.

Removing Point structure, Membrane structure, port order or causal reachability produces false viability in
the dedicated pressures.

### Recursive semantic authority interfaces must be complete

Every exact open ingress and egress Strand must occur exactly once on the recurrence semantic boundary.
This prevents hidden authority requirements or unbounded open-authority accumulation from escaping the
fixed recursive interface. Client Presentation remains a separate non-authoritative concern.

### Theory now includes authority-support occurrences

Boundary Theory is no longer projected only to public value slots. It is indexed over the union of public
value Points and exact Points supporting the open authority boundary. Correlations across those two kinds
of coordinate survive as one positive relation.

At live Judgement, a Rule fact on an authority Point can be established, contradicted or left Unknown by
knowledge attached to the exact selected live Point. Unknown remains viable; contradiction eliminates the
branch.

### Construction correspondence performs the exact pullback

Rule authority Points are related to live occurrence Points only by native Worlds Construction quotient
evidence. One Rule Point joined to two live Points constrains both. Two Rule Points joined through one
shared live Point must be Theory-compatible. No alias analysis is introduced.

### Existential hiding remains essential

Knowledge on a closed internal Point disappears from the boundary case, but a hidden witness which entails
a public authority-boundary fact leaves that consequence behind. This is the same regular-doctrine law
found by the restrictive Theory experiments, now exercised inside recursive Geometry viability.

### Recursive knowledge transitions can stabilise authority recursion

A preferred recursive authority step may be valid under current exact occurrence knowledge and publish
knowledge which makes the preferred child recurrence impossible. The resulting finite recursive descendant
can then be proved stable through ordinary Development composition.

### Hostile exact composition does not require a larger quotient

The final pressure pass added the cases most likely to distinguish hidden history: an independently captured
scarce Strand merged after a child; split/merge shells straddling recurrence; lawful leave-and-return locality
excursions; a feedback SCC whose internal authority is annihilated after Theory crosses an exact cut; mutually
recursive authority transfer; and cross-root `or_else` whose viability depends jointly on Geometry and Theory.

All remain inside the same boundary-Development quotient. Explicit capture cannot distinguish two child
Developments already equal at that boundary. Split/merge recurrence saturates once its public causal relation
is established. Internal locality excursions disappear while actual public locality movement remains visible.
Feedback closure transports positive knowledge before hiding the internal authority occurrence. Mutual SCCs
collapse by the same finite cases, and recursive priority still consults those paired cases rather than separate
Geometry and Theory summaries.

### Search remains disposable

Semantic viability saturates by boundary cases rather than recursive history depth. Discovery order and
finite search frontier are irrelevant to the fixed point. Package roundtrip and separately compiled semantic
roots preserve the same authority/knowledge Judgement.

## Scope and caveat

The boundary quotient is an executable candidate supported by the current pressure suite, not a proof of
full abstraction for every possible Worlds extension. In particular, future carrier kinds or new observable
boundary structure would have to re-earn the quotient laws.

Within current Worlds Geometry, however, the pressures found no need for a parallel authority-effect
language. The smallest successful evidence remained a quotient of Development itself.
