# Concurrency discovery pressure map

The arena exists to falsify the claim that Relay needs a separate concurrency semantics. A pressure
should add expressive reach while trying hard not to add semantic species.

## Established pressures

- synchronous two-party rendezvous from ordinary open sibling closure;
- exact scarce-authority transfer through the same rendezvous;
- multi-party triple swap from pairwise cuts only;
- `choice`, `or_else`, `and_then`, `each`, `together`, `form` and `perform` remain universal;
- dependent RHS formation from tentative LHS knowledge;
- descendant-stable preference across dynamically formed continuations;
- Strand-supported Theory transported through annihilating Construction correspondence;
- cell typestate/scarcity as ordinary open state transition;
- stable knowledge fan-out without exact state capture;
- heterogeneous channel/cell `choice`, `or_else` and `together` without adapters;
- positive occurrence knowledge does not itself authorise closure;
- direct cell transition cycles expose the persistent-state grounding problem rather than being
  hidden by a Cell-specific exception;
- grounded IVar/MVar owner transitions prevent request self-support while retaining ordinary open
  client events;
- a one-place buffered channel is derived directly from MVar put/take;
- channel receive, MVar take and a performed Form coexist in one ordinary choice family;
- freshly minted imported exact Points give dynamic endpoint identity without string names.

## Established second-wave pressures

- productive cyclic Package topology for persistent owner state;
- recurrence transport of Strand-supported Theory and surviving open Presentation;
- portable symbolic Point parameters instantiated by rigid exact imported Points;
- semaphore capacity as scarce token topology;
- countdown latch completion as positive all-token evidence;
- directed fixed-arity barrier synchronisation distinct from rendezvous atomicity;
- compare/exchange as positive equality plus `or_else` fallback;
- actor turn from MVar mailbox/state plus dependent composition;
- cancellation as consumption of residual exact authority;
- unresolved external readiness as an open environmental boundary;
- known cyclic turn-taking without scheduler state, with eventuality kept as a liveness obligation;
- per-location atomic order without global SC, and release/acquire as explicit causal authority.

## Immediate next pressures

1. **Six-party triple-swap pool** — establish two simultaneous triples from one participant pool.
   This remains an algorithmic canonicalisation/search pressure, not a reason to add runtime groups.
2. **Membrane-local actor/mailbox** — place recurrent state and mailbox inside a locality boundary and
   pressure migration/portal/handoff without an Actor carrier.
3. **Structured cancellation** — propagate cancellation through nested Forms, `and_then`, `choice` and
   recurrent scopes while preserving scarcity and branch correlation.
4. **Liveness obligations** — represent promised progress separately from finite possibility/viability,
   then show a known cyclic realisation can discharge an obligation without a general scheduler.
5. **Weak-memory outcomes** — move beyond ordering edges to litmus-test families for C11/Wasm-style
   allowed outcomes, keeping semantic causality distinct from physical implementation.
6. **Dynamic pools / fan-in / fan-out** — preserve genuine partner ambiguity without search-order
   commitment and without enumerating symmetric identities unnecessarily.
7. **Back residualisation** — only after the pressures above, measure exactly what unresolved
   coordination, liveness policy and memory-order machinery crosses the airlock.

## Constitution

A pressure has failed if the easiest implementation merely introduces an authoritative
`Scheduler`, `Task`, `Blocked`, `ChannelState`, `CellState`, `SelectState` or other
concurrency-specific semantic object without demonstrating why Development + Theory + Family cannot
express the needed fact.
