# Results

The arena currently has fifty executable pressures.

## Open-boundary representation

Established mechanically:

- the earlier `match signature` object is no longer needed;
- open composition uses ordinary exact authority Strands selected by a small open-boundary
  Presentation;
- positive protocol/value meaning is supported on the Strand occurrences themselves;
- selected cuts transport that meaning through `Construction:correspondence()` before vanished
  classes are existentially hidden;
- this remains correct when rendezvous annihilates the source Strand and `Construction:image` is
  legitimately `nil`;
- a non-channel signal/wait protocol composes through the same mechanism;
- positive supported knowledge alone does not grant permission for sibling closure.

A separate point-incidence prototype was rejected: encoding protocol description as extra Points
made descriptive matching state part of exact Geometry and amplified canonical symmetry.

- whole-network and staged cooperative discovery erase to identical Package meaning;
- independent rendezvous remain causally incomparable;

- constructing that Form adds no causality, while consumed exact captures do;

## Cells and heterogeneous democracy

The first cell pressures establish several distinctions:

- with an explicit initial state, Empty -> put -> Full -> take -> Empty is ordinary directed
  Geometry;
- cell identity and typestate are positive occurrence meaning rather than primitive checks;
- one exact state occurrence cannot satisfy two destructive takes;
- stable positive knowledge about a full value may feed several readers without capturing state
  authority or adding causal edges;
- a linear read which consumes/reproduces state authority serialises and yields the two lawful
  causal orders;
- one `together` may contain an independent channel rendezvous and cell-state chain without
  serialising them;
- one ordinary `choice` may offer either channel receive or cell take, and the available environment
  selects the complete descendant;
- heterogeneous `or_else` uses the same stable-refutation rule and leaves no facility-specific
  dominance machinery.

The direct cell encoding also intentionally exposes a falsifier: `put` and `take` alone can form a
self-supporting SCC without an initial state. Worlds is correctly solving the geometry it was given.

The grounded-owner follow-up then establishes:

- an empty IVar cannot satisfy `await` before fulfilment;
- one IVar `put` advances Empty to Full and a second `put` has no descendant;
- several stable Full observations reuse positive knowledge without consuming state authority or
  manufacturing Faces;
- MVar Empty/Full state can be threaded repeatedly through the same owner family;
- bare MVar client requests cannot self-ground;
- a one-place buffered channel is exactly MVar `put` / `take`, and a send may complete before its
  later receive;
- channel receive, MVar take and a computation produced by `form` / `perform` inhabit one ordinary
  `choice` family with no `SelectState` ontology.


## Exact dynamic identity

A separate exact-side pressure mints endpoint identity with `W.minter()`. The minted Point is
imported into send/receive seam incidence rather than owned by either relative action. Worlds'
existing rigid-import rule for `Close` then gives nominal behaviour mechanically:

- independently minted Points remain distinct even when given the same presentation name;
- same-Point send/receive has one complete rendezvous and SCC-normalises to one joint Face;
- different-Point send/receive has no closure solution;
- the dynamic identity is not retained as internal result Geometry after the seam is closed.

The important representation consequence is that dynamic endpoint identity belongs on the exact
instantiation side. Portable Package meaning should parameterise it rather than serialise it.
