# Findings after hostile expansion

## 1. Occurrence-relative Theory survives the main reconstruction pressures

Two states may have the same exact Geometry while carrying different relations
between exact occurrences.  The generated correlation atlas exercises all 24
pairings of four roots/supports over one identical Geometry and makes 384
contextual probes; every intended pairing remains distinguishable without
turning the relation into scarce authority.

Shared alpha-only existential variables are sufficient to keep multiple facts
of one logical record correlated.  They have no operational authority and their
names are observationally alpha-only.

## 2. Independent descriptive correlation does not imply shared authority

Three separately prepared member edits of one record commit in all six orders.
Three separately prepared edits of three different records sharing one root also
commit in all six orders while retaining each record's untouched sibling slot.

Thus correlation may be semantically consequential and still non-scarce.

## 4. Theory staleness is real and distinct from authority staleness

A candidate prepared with still-live exact authority can later fail because its
non-scarce Theory premise was retracted by another commit.  `commit` therefore
rechecks Theory against the current state.  Conversely, equal-looking Theory
never rescues a consumed Strand: exact Worlds staleness remains authoritative.

## 5. Quotienting can create semantic contradictions

Two individually consistent functional facts can become inconsistent when their
exact carrier keys are identified by a Worlds Construction.  Extended Theory
validates rebased facts after the exact quotient rather than assuming
construction preserves consistency automatically.

The complementary hiding pressure is also maintained: full relational coherence
is checked before boundary projection, so consumed evidence cannot hide an
incoherent construction.

without imposing causal order on that work.

The expanded evidence supports a narrower and stronger hypothesis than the
factor-shadow architecture:

> **Geometry carries exact participation, scarcity, causality and locality.**
> **Occurrence-relative Theory states non-scarce relational meaning about exact**
> **participants.  Exact Worlds Construction rebases both coherently; relational**

second operational carrier.  The harder remaining question is representation:

exact lift may be two implementations of the same broader Theory.
