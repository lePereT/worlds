# Theory speculation

**Status: non-authoritative executable research.**

This programme asks what Worlds Theory may describe without enlarging the
operational Geometry ontology.

The working discipline is:

```text
Geometry     exact participation, scarcity, causality and locality
Theory       non-scarce truth about exact participants
Judgement    finite knowledge/search about proposed composition
```

A Theory experiment may refer to operational carriers, but merely referring to
a carrier must never create, duplicate, consume, relocate or otherwise confer
authority over it. Exact operational change remains Worlds construction.

## Occurrence-relative Theory

`occurrence_relative/` is the current hostile programme. It asks whether Theory
indexed by an exact Geometry may refer to Points, Strands, Membranes and Faces,
plus alpha-only logical variables, while remaining non-authoritative.

The suite pressures exact correlation, split/merge, independent preparation,
hiding, support, feedback/SCCs, fresh instantiation, long-running renewal,
scarcity, and Construction-image rebasing. It deliberately leaves support,
causal order and scarcity in Geometry rather than encoding them again in Theory.

Run it with:

```sh
./speculation/theory/occurrence_relative/run.sh
```

The older `speculation/modern/theory_algebras.lua` experiment is complementary:
it varies elimination algebras over fixed incidence shape. It remains in
`modern/` because it belongs to that factor-incidence programme rather than this
carrier-relative Theory programme.
