# Speculation

Worlds 0.6.3 keeps the verified Geometry centre unchanged and promotes only
Construction and Presentation at the semantic edge.  The substantially richer
dimensional/inter-World work below remains speculation.

Earlier experiments often treated quantum phase, tensor cycles, overlapping
contexts and symbolic elimination as pressure for a larger Geometry. The newer
experiments found a more economical possibility: important non-causal structure
is already present in ordered Point-Strand incidence, while Strand-Face incidence
carries causal realisation and Membranes carry hierarchical support. Theory then
supplies domain algebra and extensional truth rather than compensating for an
otherwise missing generic factor graph.

The new default rule is therefore:

> Before enlarging Worlds, try to derive the required distinction from an
> interaction among existing incidences. Add ontology only when a new distinction
> in causal permission or exact operational identity survives hostile attempts to
> express it with the present four carriers.

This is a research discipline, not a law.

Run all executable experiments from the repository root with:

```sh
make speculation-check
```

The corpus has no Python/SymPy dependency. Experiments which mention a known
symbolic elimination result compare against a pre-determined result rather than
calling an external CAS.

## Modern programme

The primary current experiments live under `modern/`.

- `modern/factor_incidence.lua` — treats face-free Point-Strand incidence as
  ordered factor/configuration structure. Exercises shared and repeated
  coordinates, cyclic tensor networks without causal cycles, generic contraction
  matching, elimination fill-in, membrane interaction and the geometric limit of
  quantum separability.
- `modern/latent_structures.lua` — explores endpoint locality/support patterns,
  structural Strand rows with exact scarce occurrence fibres, and the
  owned/local versus imported/rigid Point binding/freshness lifecycle.
- `modern/join_structures.lua` — attacks join as congruence generation, exact
  identity transport and frame/template polarity.
- `modern/theory_algebras.lua` — holds one factor-incidence shape fixed while
  changing the external elimination algebra: Boolean existential composition,
  min-plus, sum-product and a pre-determined polynomial elimination result.
- `modern/membrane_cross_axis.lua` — demonstrates the genuine laminar limit of
  one membrane tree using crossing host/security contexts and the alternative of
  leaving the second axis as external selection/structure.
- `modern/quantum_two_layer.lua` — separates scarce causal quantum authority from
  amplitude-factor scope over the same Points. Geometry can expose a candidate
  factorisation; a tiny external amplitude Theory distinguishes product from Bell
  structure.

These experiments are intentionally small. Their purpose is to falsify
interpretations of the existing kernel, not to establish production domain
libraries.

### Dimensional laboratory and shadows

`modern/dimensional_lab/` is the current broad pressure programme.  It includes
cross-domain rank/configuration experiments plus `shadows/`, where fixed lower
shadows are held constant while higher fillings, causal polarity, Membrane
support and exact construction history vary.  `shadows/fibre_census/` studies
the topology of those realisation fibres and the causal-spine support law;
`shadows/between_worlds/` tests structural deformation, causal meta-rewrite,
construction-image coherence, provenance and symmetry between exact Worlds.

These experiments motivate the 0.6.3 production split but are **not** production
semantics.  In particular they do not establish public W1/W3 types, a canonical
rank, a canonical inter-World adjacency or a higher-cell carrier.

## Updated reading of tensors and elimination

The old `research_excursions.lua` contained a useful but limited hostile example:
a tensor cycle encoded directly as a causal Face cycle is SCC-normalised. In
0.6.2 that result is retained as a category-error warning rather than treated as
evidence for a missing tensor geometry.

The modern reading is:

```text
Point-Strand incidence   tensor/factor topology
Strand-Face incidence    selected contraction/evaluation schedule
Theory                   scalar algebra and extensional equality
```

Cyclic tensor topology is therefore ordinary face-free Geometry; only a cyclic
*causal schedule* is an SCC.

## Updated reading of Theory

Theory is no longer treated merely as an opaque escape hatch. Point-Strand
incidence can expose which exact variables/coordinates occur together, allowing
domain consequence to remain local.

A generic hidden-Point step has the shape:

```text
collect incident factor Strands
        |
        v
choose surviving ordered scope
        |
        v
realise with a Face
        |
        v
Theory determines elimination / consequence
```

The same structural step can be decorated by existential quantification,
sum-product, min-plus, algebraic elimination or another domain operation.

## Quantum: atlas and classical-correspondence programmes

### Current primary line

The 0.6.2 programme no longer treats "quantum" as one missing geometric axis.
The atlas experiments instead ask which already-independent Worlds structures
support different quantum phenomena:

```text
contextuality          overlapping Point-Strand scopes
identical particles    exact occurrence fibres + permutation semantics
interference           exact histories + observational boundary classes
phase                  gauge-like weights on transport/history
classicality           Theory-certified copyability of explicit processes
process semantics      join / juxtaposition interpreted as composition / tensor
locality               scarce local authority + extensional global state
```

The important correction to the earlier two-layer toy is that a quantum state or
amplitude factor should **not automatically become a scarce Strand** merely
because its scope can be written over Points. The hostile locality experiment
shows why: consuming a root-supported joint state Strand makes an update
root-supported even when the physical intervention uses only Alice-local
authority. The current split is therefore more disciplined:

- subsystem/index identity may be represented by Points;
- actual scarce causal authority may be represented by exact Strands;
- exact factor/resources may also be Strands when they really are operational
  occurrences;
- gates, measurements and other interventions may be represented by Faces;
- overlapping compatibility/factor scopes may be exposed by Point-Strand
  incidence without becoming causal succession;
- global quantum state, amplitudes, phase, unitarity, separability and Born
  semantics may remain Theory unless their exact operational occurrence itself
  matters.

No fifth quantum carrier is currently motivated.

### Quantum-to-classical correspondence

A stronger 0.6.2 line now asks the reverse question: if ordinary Worlds process
shapes admit a compositional quantum semantics, can recognisably classical
semantics arise *inside* it rather than being supplied as an unrelated second
interpretation?

Ten dependency-free experiments under `modern/quantum_classical/` currently
exercise distinct routes:

- causal hiding paired with quantum partial trace gives a dephased surviving
  state, while coherent uncomputation before hiding restores coherence;
- dephasing fixed points are ordinary probability vectors and preserving quantum
  channels restrict to stochastic/Markov maps;
- explicit split geometry copies a preferred classical basis but entangles a
  superposition;
- environment coupling selects the copyable/pointer basis without changing the
  Worlds process shape;
- one system can generate many exact membrane-separated records carrying one
  stable classical pointer fact while proper fragments cannot see global phase;
- commuting quantum contexts yield local classical outcome spaces whose gluing
  can fail contextually;
- a decoherence functional becomes an additive probability measure on suitable
  observational history classes;
- Hong-Ou-Mandel-style exchange recovers classical distinguishable-particle
  statistics when exact identity remains observable at the boundary;
- coherent hidden routes can sum to an exactly deterministic classical map even
  though no individual route is deterministic;
- a finite stationary-phase toy makes a least-action intermediate history
  dominate as action/hbar grows.

These results do not identify `boundary` with decoherence, derive the Born rule,
solve measurement, or derive classical mechanics. Their value is the convergent
possibility that different pieces of classical structure correspond to different
existing Worlds mechanisms: fixed points, quotients, copyability, exact
redundancy, locality, observational hiding and coherent cancellation.

The dedicated non-authoritative note
`docs/QUANTUM-CLASSICAL-CORRESPONDENCE.md` records the early correspondence,
its stronger hypothetical interpretation, comparison targets and explicit
falsifiers.

  which-path records and coherent erasure.

  phase falsifies raw-Geometry boundary sufficiency for quantum prediction.
- `quantum/worlds_quantum_contextual_boundary_test.lua` — contextual-equivalence

- `quantum/worlds_quantised_kernel_toy.lua` and
  `worlds_quantum_kernel_explorations_4..7.lua` — historical attempts to quantise
  witness choice, locality, causal order and fresh identity themselves.

Those kernel-quantisation experiments remain hostile evidence, not the preferred
roadmap. Their strongest surviving lesson is that exact branch identity,
gauge/renaming and support cannot be ignored by any future quantum
interpretation.

## Chemistry

- `chemistry/worlds_chemistry_toy.lua` — open compartmental reaction networks
  over exact scarce molecule authority. It exposes the symmetry factor between
  exact ordered matchings and chemically unordered opportunities.

The 0.6.2 fibre interpretation sharpens this result: exact molecule occurrences
form a scarce fibre over a structural row, while chemistry may quotient the
permutation action of indistinguishable members for rate semantics. The quotient
must not erase exact causal authority.

## Logic

- `logic/worlds_linear_logic_toy.lua` — multiplicative linear process skeleton:
  identity as continuity, solve/join as linear composition, juxtaposition,
  exchange, no free contraction and no free weakening.

The 0.6.2 binding/fibre observations add a second possible line of attack:
persistent Point/Membrane context may be compared with non-linear structural
variables while exact Strands provide linear occurrences. This remains a
research analogy, not a claim that a complete linear/dependent logic has been
recovered.

## Adversarial

- `adversarial/worlds_bodyline_tests.lua` — deliberately hostile tests against
  stronger extrapolations: the unqualified "2-complex" description, retained
  naked Point references, factorial exact-witness symmetry, non-injective local
  Point/Membrane matching, overlapping context, raw-boundary minimality, SCC
  topology erasure and scarcity-vs-conservation.

The modern programme treats these negative results as first-class evidence. In
particular, one membrane tree really cannot represent two crossing enclosing
context systems, and raw Geometry alone really cannot decide phase,
separability or arbitrary domain equality.

## Status

Passing these scripts means only that their internal assertions hold for the toy
constructions. It does not promote their interpretations to Worlds laws, proof
claims or API commitments.
