# Transactional Recurrence Calculus — results

The package-pure recurrence model survives 32 pressures with 269 direct assertions.

## Main result

The finite transactional calculus does not need a recursive evaluator extension.

A genuinely unbounded recursive element can be represented as a finite cyclic Package graph whose
nodes are open relative Development contexts. Every node is symbolic Worlds Geometry plus positive
Theory and marked interfaces. The graph edge which ties a hole to another semantic root is Package
structure only.

The denotation is the least positive fixed point of this finite equation system. Every finite member
of that denotation is already one ordinary relative Development.

## Arbitrary compilation scale is decisive

For the unary authority recurrence `Loop = identity OR step ; Loop`, depth `n+1` contains exactly the
finite chains with zero through `n` Faces. For every tested chain through seven Faces, the member
obtained by recursive unfolding is byte-identical to compiling that whole finite chain directly with
the finite transactional calculus.

Separately compiling the recursive library and a dependent wrapper, linking them by semantic root,
and then unfolding also produces the same bytes as compiling both definitions jointly from the
start.

This gives concrete evidence for the scale-invariance principle:

> larger-scale Relay compilation can consume recursive Package structure and emit the same species
> of finite Development meaning as direct finite compilation.

## Recursion found and repaired a finite normal-form defect

The first version of this test failed at a three-Face authority chain. The Geometry was exactly
isomorphic, but symbolic Strand numbering still reflected composition history.

That was a real violation of the old Relay Package promise. The finite laboratory now performs a
structural Geometry alpha-normalisation using public authority marks, colour refinement and bounded
symmetry search. Its own suite grows from 29 to 30 pressures and from 278 to 300 direct assertions.
Left- and right-associated authority composition now agree through eight tested stages, and the
recursive/direct scale comparisons agree through seven.

## Recursion is Package topology, not Theory

The new model deliberately does not resurrect old Relay's `invoke` relation inside Theory.

A recursive hole is marked boundary in a Development context. The Package graph says which semantic
root fills it. Filling the hole is ordinary positive composition: exact Strand cuts for authority,
positive equality/projection for value knowledge, then existential removal of the internal boundary.

This keeps the restrictive-Theory direction intact. Theory describes knowledge carried through the
recursive boundary; it does not mean "call this definition".

## Tail and non-tail recurrence need no stack ontology

Tail recurrence is repeated filling of a context whose continuation side is empty.

Non-tail recurrence uses the same mechanism with wrapper Geometry on both sides of the hole. The
laboratory produces finite meanings with zero, two, four, six and eight wrapper Faces without ever
introducing a stack, continuation record or recursive runtime node into Package meaning.

Whether Back later realises a residual recurrent boundary as a jump, loop, stack frame, heap closure,
worker event or nothing at all remains physicalisation.

## Authority laws survive recurrence

A unary scarce authority passed through recursion forms the expected exact causal chain. No
"linear-recursion" feature exists.

One wrapper output cannot be assigned to two recursive holes: context validation rejects the attempt
before unfolding. Two genuinely independent recursive authority channels, by contrast, produce
Cartesian sibling meanings and preserve incomparable Faces rather than being serialised.

Thus continuation multiplicity continues to follow exact authority topology.

## Positive knowledge can drive recurrence

The inherited tiny equality Theory can distinguish a grounded base branch from a recursive branch,
feed a different positive value into the recursive hole, and recover the resulting output knowledge.
No callback is required to choose or construct the descendant.

This is only a small model, but it supports the intended future integration with the restrictive
knowledge doctrine: recurrence consumes certified positive knowledge and ordinary exact authority,
not arbitrary evaluator state.

## Preference exposes a new infinite-family law

The most important new semantic pressure concerns `or_else`.

If `recurse` dominates `stop`, each finite approximant has a frontier consisting of its deepest
completed recursive descendant. That frontier changes at every depth. In the infinite least fixed
point, every finite candidate is dominated by a deeper finite candidate, so there is no undominated
finite member.

Therefore bounded unfolding cannot itself determine commit eligibility. A searcher must not commit a
candidate merely because it is on the frontier of the finite prefix currently explored.

The dual pressure also passes: when the grounded base dominates recurrence, the base remains the
stable frontier at every deeper approximant.

This appears to be a useful semantic criterion for any future recursive Judgement algorithm.

## Nonproductive recursion is semantically empty but not cheaply refutable

A pure cycle with no grounded alternative has no member in any finite approximant, so its least
finite denotation is empty.

That is different from old Relay's operational behaviour, where exhausting recursive expansion
reported `Unknown`. The new distinction is cleaner: semantic denotation may be empty while a bounded
solver is unable to establish that fact. Solver exhaustion should therefore be a computational
`Deferred`/resource result rather than semantic `Unknown` or an unjustified `Retry`.

## Semantic roots are stronger than source definition identity

Recursive SCCs are canonicalised without local source names. Renaming or reordering mutually
recursive definitions preserves roots and Package bytes. Two completely symmetric recursive entry
points can even hash-cons to the same semantic root because the Package contains no semantic fact
which distinguishes them.

This is slightly purer than old Relay's source-identity-preserving SCC roots while retaining the old
properties that matter: finite canonical transport, content-derived roots, separate linking and fresh
exact instantiation.

## Remaining work

The largest semantic gap is now full restrictive Theory integration. This laboratory inherits only
the finite equality doctrine. Real occurrence-supported domain knowledge must be transported through
native Construction correspondence and existential hiding at recursive boundaries.

A second gap is higher-order recurrence: recursive closures whose environments themselves contain
semantic roots and exact captured authority. The current context model appears capable of carrying
those interfaces, but that claim has not been pressured.

A production judge for recursive Packages is also intentionally absent. The moving-frontier result
shows that such a judge must reason about stability/productivity rather than simply unfolding until a
current winner appears.

Finally, the SCC and Geometry canonicalisers use deliberately expensive research algorithms. Their
laws are the result; their implementations are not proposed production machinery.
