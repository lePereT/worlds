# Restrictive Theory follow-up

Status: research only. No production Worlds source is changed.

This lab follows two results from the restrictive Theory audit:

1. explicit Theory retraction may be unnecessary if mutable knowledge has exact lifecycle support;
2. the richer Construction correspondence required by compositional knowledge transport may be the
   same provenance structure whose surviving shadow generated the native between-Worlds groupoid.

The lab deliberately tests both claims against current Worlds rather than changing the kernel first.

## Experiment A: positive Theory with exact support

`positive_theory.lua` is the existing extended-Theory experimental engine with the semantic retract
channel removed. `T.rule` rejects a `retracts` field.

`01_support_lifetime.lua` pressures the intended lifecycle law:

- contradictory functional knowledge about one surviving Point is rejected;
- compatible knowledge on the same surviving exact support may accumulate;
- mutable state is represented by a Strand occurrence which is consumed and replaced by a successor;
- old knowledge disappears from the public State when its exact support is no longer on the boundary;
- a previously prepared Rule whose Theory premise lost its support becomes stale even while unrelated
  exact authority survives.

`run_legacy_48.lua` runs all 48 existing extended-Theory hostile pressures through a compatibility
adapter which erases their legacy retract lists before the strict positive engine sees the Rule. All
48 still pass.

This proves only that the retract channel is redundant for those pressures. The positive engine is
otherwise the old hostile-lab Theory implementation; in particular its boundary projector is not the
new exact regular-doctrine implementation.

## Experiment B: one Construction correspondence

`correspondence.lua` defines a research-only immutable equivalence correspondence over tagged source
and target carrier occurrences. It is evidence around Construction, not Geometry and not authority.

For one current Construction it records:

- every surviving source-to-result image;
- exact source quotient identifications induced by boundary equations;
- the Point and Membrane identifications forced by those equations; and
- source-only quotient classes even when the final result image is absent.

The current research implementation reconstructs this from the public `parts`, `equations`, `result`
and `image` interfaces. Production Worlds is untouched. A future native implementation should retain
the kernel's actual quotient classes directly rather than rely on reconstruction.

Correspondences compose. The second Construction may consume the first target together with fresh
exact parts, which is the shape required by staged Relay transaction formation.

The same object has two uses.

First, positive knowledge can be transported by existential direct image along the correspondence.
This preserves constraints created by an intermediate identity quotient even when that quotient class
is subsequently annihilated. The final partial `Construction:image` alone cannot do this.

Second, two correspondence codomains with the same marked source induce the exact source-relative
endpoint map used by the existing between-Worlds construction-groupoid experiments.
