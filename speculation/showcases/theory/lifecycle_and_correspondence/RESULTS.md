# Results: restrictive Theory follow-up

## Result 1: semantic retraction is unnecessary in the tested Theory pressures

The strict research Theory has no retract operation.

All 48 existing extended-Theory hostile pressures still pass when their legacy retract lists are
erased before Rule construction. New direct pressures establish a stronger lifecycle discipline:
positive knowledge may accumulate on a surviving exact occurrence; contradictory functional change
on that same occurrence is rejected; mutation succeeds by consuming/renewing the exact support and
asserting knowledge about the successor occurrence.

This suggests the constitutional law:

**Theory is monotone on surviving exact support. Apparent mutable state change requires exact support
renewal.**

Boundary/liveness then determines which occurrence is current. Theory does not independently delete
old semantic facts.

The conclusion is deliberately narrower than a complete Theory redesign: these pressures still use
the older hostile-lab fact engine and its simple boundary projection. They establish redundancy of
semantic retraction, not the adequacy of that projector as exact existential semantics.

## Result 2: final Construction image is a lossy projection of exact provenance

An actual Worlds two-stage causal feedback construction gives the decisive counterexample.

Stage one identifies two distinct source Strands. Stage two forms a causal SCC which annihilates the
shared intermediate Strand. Positive source knowledge relates each of those two source Strands to a
different surviving Strand.

Staged knowledge transport remembers the intermediate equality and therefore entails a correlation
between the two survivors. Looking only at the final partial source-to-result image sees both identified
sources as `nil` and loses that correlation.

The research Construction correspondence retains the vanished source quotient class. Transport along
it agrees exactly with staged transport, while partial-image transport does not.

## Result 3: the same correspondence reproduces the native between-Worlds groupoid

The existing native construction-groupoid experiments derive endpoint maps from a common source's
composed Construction images.

The new correspondence independently produces those same endpoint maps by matching result classes by
their marked source support.

It was tested on:

- six staged orders plus one-shot materialisation of three independent acyclic cuts;
- the corresponding causal-SCC case, where two Faces contract many-to-one and cyclic Strands are
  annihilated; and
- every triple of exact-distinct endpoint Worlds.

The maps preserve Membrane ancestry, Point support, ordered Strand incidence and producer/consumer
structure, and compose exactly. The unified groupoid test performs 20,784 assertions.

So the Theory-transport correspondence and the source-relative between-Worlds correspondence are not
merely analogous in the tested cases. They are two uses of one source-to-result quotient-provenance
object.

## Result 4: correspondence composition has the shape Relay needs

The correspondence composes when a later Construction consumes the previous result together with a
fresh exact part. Its visible source becomes the original source parts plus the newly introduced part.
A staged A-to-B-to-D chain and the equivalent one-shot three-part Construction have the same marked
source quotient and source-relative endpoint correspondence.

Composition also passed direct identity and associativity pressures.

This matters because Relay transaction formation repeatedly composes an existing exact frame with new
Rule Developments. A correspondence restricted to a fixed carrier universe would not have been enough.

## Architectural interpretation

The current public `Construction:image` remains useful and should stay small. It answers the common
question: what exact result carrier, if any, is the image of this source carrier?

But it is not sufficient as the complete immutable provenance of Construction.

A richer Construction correspondence can remember exact quotient classes even when no member survives
as a result carrier. `image` is then one projection of that evidence.

The same richer evidence can support:

- exact positive Theory transport through quotient and annihilation;
- source-relative comparison of exact-distinct construction outcomes;
- compositional provenance across staged construction; and
- future higher-Worlds reasoning without inventing a new Geometry carrier.

The research implementation derives the correspondence from today's public Construction evidence. That
is evidence that the concept is compatible with the kernel, not proof that this derivation is complete
for every lawful Construction. The kernel already owns the actual disjoint-set quotient classes during
join materialisation, so a native implementation should retain those exact classes directly.

## Proposed next kernel pressure

The smallest credible production experiment is not a fifth Worlds carrier. It is richer immutable
Construction evidence.

A future Construction might expose an opaque correspondence/class interface with laws equivalent to:

- carrier kind is preserved within each class;
- surviving `image(x)` is the unique result member of x's class when one exists;
- source-only classes are retained when their result member is annihilated;
- identity and staged composition are coherent;
- source-relative codomain comparison recovers the existing construction groupoid; and
- the evidence grants no authority and cannot mint exact carriers.

The exact API should be discovered by a kernel-native differential lab rather than fixed from this
research implementation.
