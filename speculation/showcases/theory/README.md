# Restrictive Theory showcase

The current Theory line asks a deliberately narrow question: what positive
knowledge can live over exact Worlds occurrences without becoming an escape
hatch for authority, causality or arbitrary execution?

## Candidate constitution

The strongest current model is a bottomed regular knowledge doctrine indexed by
exact Worlds occurrence contexts:

- knowledge combines monotonically by positive conjunction;
- Hit, Retry and semantic Unknown derive from the information order;
- exact identity and substitution come from Worlds;
- hidden exact occurrences are forgotten by existential projection, not by
  fact-by-fact deletion;
- Construction transport uses the native quotient correspondence, including
  classes which are identified and later annihilated;
- interacting domains close by positive monotone fixed point;
- observations require replayable positive certificates and confer no authority;
- solver exhaustion is Deferred, not semantic Unknown or Retry.

Knowledge is monotone on surviving exact support. Apparent mutation is modelled
by exact occurrence renewal: when a supporting Strand is consumed, knowledge
attached to that occurrence disappears through boundary projection and new
positive knowledge can belong to its successor. There is no semantic retract
channel.

## Laboratories

`knowledge_contract/` contains the algebraic falsification suite: meet/order,
existential hiding, Frobenius and Beck-Chevalley pressures, Construction
transport, corelation-style composition, positive closure and certified
observation. The packaged self-contained suite has 1,645 assertions.

`lifecycle_and_correspondence/` connects those laws back to exact Worlds. It
pressures support lifetime without retraction and shows that one Construction
correspondence serves both compositional Theory transport and the source-relative
between-Worlds groupoid discovered independently in dimensional speculation.

These laboratories do not make Theory a Worlds carrier. Their negative result is
important: arbitrary non-authoritative relations cannot be encoded faithfully by
Membrane, Point, Strand or Face without changing their meaning.
