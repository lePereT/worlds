# Research notes — Worlds 0.3

0.3 tests a stronger hypothesis than 0.2: once complete attachment is defined
over an available Frontier rather than only current actuality, exact residual
development may already contain the geometry usually introduced separately as
causality, conflict and true concurrency.

## Result so far

Executable tests support the following derived readings:

```text
causality
    consumption of exact symbolic egress/fresh identity

local conflict
    exact occurrences are individually possible but fail coherent joint
    residual completion

independence
    exact occurrences form a coherent residual cube
```

The directed suite derives 2-, 3- and 5-dimensional cells without carrier cube
objects. Generated grid tests compare exact pointed cuts with an independent
set-theoretic oracle.

## Character of the geometry

The resulting structure is closer to a higher-dimensional transition geometry
than to a prime event structure. Conflict is local and need not be hereditary:
a competing event can consume a resource and produce a successor resource that
enables a fresh later occurrence.

## Compiler consequence

A certified Reader is a valid source for `Att`. A compiler Back can therefore
explore exact residual semantic futures directly from a nameless certificate,
without implementing a second matching or control-flow reconstruction system.

## Open research

The principal mathematical work is now formalisation: prove the correspondence
between exact residual Frontiers/Cuts and a suitable precubical or related
higher-dimensional structure; characterise the conditions under which cubical
completion is unique; and establish graft/certification preservation formally.

Resource participants, Fibers and Relay remain intended falsification clients,
not kernel ontology.
