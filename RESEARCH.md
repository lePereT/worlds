# Research notes — Worlds 0.2

The research claim is not that graphs, linear resources, fresh names or open systems are individually new. The interesting possibility is that this particular factorisation explains many things normally given separate semantic machinery.

```text
World    where authority is local and where actuality grows
Point    what identity is involved
Strand   which occurrence / possession exists
Face     what transformation happens
```

0.2 adds no carrier object. It changes how possibility becomes actual:

```text
detached open patch P
+
actual boundary K
        ↓
     Att_K(P)
        ↓
 complete witness
        ↓
      graft
```

## Why Att(K) matters

Computational experiments showed that trigger-first local matching confuses search state with geometric truth. A locally ambiguous early demand can be globally unique; it can also belong to a globally empty attachment space.

The complete attachment relation therefore looks like a more appropriate mathematical object than a trigger-centred transition rule.

## Derived concurrency hypothesis

Given exact residual attachment, the experiments currently classify pairs as follows:

```text
both residuals exist and commute   independence
only one causal order exists       causal dependence
both single actions exist but
no joint residual exists           conflict
```

Resource-disjoint families generated coherent squares, cubes and a tested 5-cube without adding concurrency metadata to the carrier.

The research question is whether `Att` canonically derives a cubical/configuration structure and what class of such structures lies in its image.

Do not add `independent`, `conflict`, `parallel` or cube objects to Worlds merely to make this statement true.

## Open process hypothesis / Fibers

The Fibers V1 experiments suggest:

```text
choice      alternative complete attachments
and_then    causal composition while detached
each        symmetric independent product / higher cell
together    symmetric open-process gluing + hiding + resource theory
or_else     certified emptiness of the preferred attachment family
```

A major constitutional result from those experiments is:

> sibling order is not causal order.

Resource theories may have noncommutative causal `and_then` evolution, but sibling contribution under `together` must be symmetric; meaningful order must be explicit geometry/data.

For rendezvous, exhaustive tests matched Fibers `together` exactly with compatible port wiring followed by hiding and causal-acyclicity checking. Stateful theories additionally require a coherent partial sibling-composition algebra.

## Quantum direction

The same process/configuration geometry may admit non-classical enrichments without changing the carrier. Early experiments separate two axes:

```text
higher geometry    relations/transformations between processes

enrichment         probability, complex amplitude, operators, quantum channels
```

Entanglement and measurement appear to belong primarily to enrichment. Higher-order processes such as the quantum switch may eventually motivate genuinely higher process structure.

This remains research; Worlds 0.2 is not a quantum formalism.

## Compiler consequences under test

If attachment/residual structure formalises cleanly, several compiler properties may be semantic consequences rather than separate analyses:

* schedule-independent parallelism from commuting cells;
* local invalidation from proof/frontier dependency;
* semantic ABI stability from canonical boundaries;
* erasure of interior semantic structure when only the boundary is observable;
* target-specific scheduling of one semantic concurrent cell.

## Research posture

The kernel should remain easy to falsify.

Hard cases should either:

```text
fall out of existing geometry
strengthen a general law
or remain explicitly unresolved
```

They should not disappear into feature-specific flags to preserve the aesthetic of a small model.
