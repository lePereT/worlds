# Live computation is boundary computation

A historical Geometry may contain arbitrarily much closed causal interior. None
of that interior is required to decide the next development.

The world contribution to `solve` is exactly its outgoing counted incidence
section. Consequently a suspended solve retains no historical Faces, consumed
Strands or Geometry shell from the world.

`boundary(G)` is the Geometry needed for the next `join`. It retains exactly:

* every exact egress Strand;
* every owned Point incident on those Strands; and
* the owned membrane ancestry required to interpret their incidence.

Surviving carriers are the same objects. The projection is idempotent:

```text
∂(∂G) = ∂G
```

Live evolution is:

```text
solve(outgoing-section(world), development)
             |
          equations
             |
             v
join({boundary(world), development}, equations)
             |
             v
          boundary
```

For bounded frontier and bounded development size, persistent operational state
therefore remains bounded independently of elapsed causal history.

`worlds.history` is optional observation. It records transition equations and
instantiated Faces but never participates in a later `solve`.
