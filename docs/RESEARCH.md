# Research

**Status: non-authoritative research ledger for Worlds 0.6.2.**

Worlds 0.6.2 changes no kernel law, public API or implementation. This research
ledger records a substantial change in interpretation after sustained pressure
from Relay and from the speculative corpus.

The default research move is now **subtractive**: before adding a carrier,
relation or more permissive membrane structure, first ask whether the required
distinction is already present in an interaction among Point-Strand incidence,
Strand-Face causality, membrane support, exact occurrence fibres, ownership,
join congruence, frame preservation and identity transport.

See `INCIDENCE-ATLAS.md` for the current inventory.

## Central 0.6.2 hypothesis

A growing class of domains appears to decompose into three layers:

```text
1. exact structural scope     existing Worlds incidence
2. extensional mathematics   Theory / domain interpretation
3. causal realisation        Faces chosen or introduced by computation
```

The research question is not whether Worlds can encode these domains in the
weak universal-computation sense. It is whether their natural decomposition is
*derived* by the existing kernel: shared exact coordinates appear as Points,
exact factor/resource occurrences as Strands, structural elimination as Faces,
and domain-specific consequence as Theory.

Evidence must distinguish explanatory reduction from mere representability.

## Point-Strand factor/configuration geometry

Face-free Geometry already supports arbitrary overlapping ordered scopes:

```text
A(i,k)   B(k,j)
```

share exact Point `k` without asserting causal succession. Repeated incidence
such as `A(i,i)` retains diagonal/multiplicity information, and cyclic factor
networks need not form causal SCCs.

This suggests a generic elimination programme:

1. choose an internal Point;
2. collect incident live Strands;
3. form the surviving ordered scope;
4. realise elimination with a Face;
5. let Theory supply the domain algebra and projected truth.

Candidate domains include tensors, finite relations, probabilistic factors,
tropical/min-cost systems, sparse linear algebra, polynomial constraints,
physical networks and symbolic inference.

A key test is whether one structural elimination planner can serve several of
these domains without knowing their algebra.

## Theory as boundary algebra, not opaque escape hatch

Theory remains essential. Geometry records factorisation/scope, not algebraic
truth. The same Point-Strand topology can carry Boolean relations, probabilities,
complex amplitudes, costs or polynomial ideals.

The target Theory contract should be investigated in terms of laws such as:

```text
nested projection
functorial remapping
locality of combination under hiding
soundness of exact or approximate consequence
```

Important distinctions include exact projection, sound over-approximation,
sound under-approximation and incomplete query procedures returning `Unknown`.

0.6.2 experiments deliberately avoid external symbolic dependencies. Where a
non-trivial algebraic elimination result is used, the script compares the
Geometry against a pre-determined expected result rather than outsourcing the
claim to SymPy or another CAS.

## Quantum programme

The older speculation explored both "quantum Theory over classical Worlds" and
"quantising Worlds itself". The 0.6.2 incidence programme now takes a more
radical but more conservative route: keep the exact kernel fixed and ask whether
different quantum phenomena are semantics on structures which Worlds already
needed for independent reasons.

The atlas-derived working map is:

```text
subsystem identity      Points
scarce physical use     exact Strand authority
intervention            Faces
compatibility scope     overlapping Point-Strand incidence
indistinguishability    symmetry over exact occurrence fibres
interference            alternative exact constructions + observation quotient
phase                    possibly gauge/holonomy data on histories/transport
entanglement             failure of Theory-certified factorisation
contextuality            failure to glue local classical subtheories globally
classical information   copyable sector of quantum semantics
```

The important negative result is locality. Representing a global entangled state
itself as one scarce joint Strand can manufacture non-local causal support: an
Alice-local update which consumes that Strand must be joint/root-supported.
Accordingly, global quantum state should normally remain extensional Theory over
exact subsystem structure unless its own operational occurrence is genuinely
scarce. Point-Strand incidence remains useful for exact factor resources and
scope, but scope alone does not imply authority.

### Classical correspondence

The programme now also asks whether ordinary classical semantics can be
*recovered from* a quantum interpretation over Worlds rather than separately
postulated.

Current experiments expose several non-equivalent classicalisation mechanisms:

1. **fixed points** — dephasing is idempotent; its fixed states are probability
   vectors and preserving quantum channels restrict to stochastic maps;
2. **copyability** — explicit split/correlation geometry copies selected pointer
   states but not arbitrary superpositions;
3. **environment selection** — interaction semantics selects which copyable
   basis/algebra is stable without changing Geometry;
4. **redundant records** — membrane-separated exact record Strands can make one
   pointer fact independently available while global phase remains hidden;
5. **local classical islands** — commuting contextual scopes possess ordinary
   sample spaces although no global valuation need exist;
6. **decoherent quotients** — exact histories acquire ordinary additive
   probabilities after suitable cross-class interference vanishes;
7. **observational distinguishability** — retaining exact labels at the boundary
   can turn exchange interference into classical distinguishable-particle
   statistics;
8. **coherent emergence of deterministic dynamics** — sums over hidden exact
   routes can cancel all classically forbidden outputs;
9. **stationary phase** — in a deliberately weak numerical toy, destructive
   cancellation suppresses non-stationary histories and selects the classical
   least-action intermediate point asymptotically.

The speculative synthesis is therefore not one quantum-to-classical map but a
possible emergence ladder:

```text
quantum semantics over exact Worlds processes
        |
        | interaction / hiding / coarse observation
        v
decoherent fixed or quotient sectors
        |
        +--> probability states + stochastic maps
        +--> copyable pointer data
        |        +--> redundant membrane-separated records
        +--> local classical contexts (which may fail to glue globally)

coherent exact histories
        +--> interference-selected deterministic laws
        +--> stationary-phase classical trajectories (asymptotic candidate)
```

The large research hypothesis is that a suitable quantum semantics might contain
something recognisably like classical Worlds process semantics as a stable
regime. If that survived formalisation, Worlds would be better viewed as an
**intensional process substrate beneath a choice of semantic regime** than as an
intrinsically classical computational ontology.

This hypothesis has a very high burden of proof. `boundary` is not thereby
physical decoherence; the experiments do not derive the Born rule, measurement
outcomes or full classical mechanics. The correspondence programme is recorded
in detail, including falsifiers, in
`QUANTUM-CLASSICAL-CORRESPONDENCE.md`.

Concrete comparison targets now include categorical quantum/process theories,
Markov/stochastic process theories, decoherent histories, sheaf/descent accounts
of contextuality, linear-resource semantics, occurrence/event structures and
open-system/cospan calculi. The goal is to determine whether Worlds supplies a
more intensional exact layer beneath several of these abstractions, not to claim
that they reduce to Worlds.

## Tensor programme

Tensor-network topology belongs primarily to Point-Strand incidence; contraction
order belongs to Face-Strand causality. This avoids the old category error in
which a cyclic tensor network was encoded as a causal cycle and therefore
correctly SCC-normalised by Worlds.

Questions:

- can generic contraction matching be expressed entirely by local Point equality
  patterns?
- can Back select contraction order from structural costs such as intermediate
  scope/treewidth?
- can physical packing, tiling, vectorisation and GEMM lowering be successive
  causal realisations over the same exact semantic Points?
- can multiple realisations preserve enough join-image evidence for explanation?

## Structural rows and exact occurrence fibres

The derived signature `(Strand membrane, ordered exact Points)` induces a
structural row whose members are exact scarce occurrences. Experiments show
falling-factorial allocation counts and exact fibre-capacity failure.

Research questions:

- what is the declarative quotient underlying the query engine's row/fibre
  factorisation?
- how should domain symmetries over indistinguishable exact occurrences be stated
  without erasing causal identity?
- is there a useful Fock-/species-/groupoid-like interpretation of exact
  multiplicity over structural rows?

## Locality and membranes

Membranes remain a forest. Experiments with crossing host/security classes show
that one tree cannot make two crossing equivalence classes simultaneous
structural enclosing contexts. This is a real restriction.

The current presumption is nevertheless to keep membranes laminar until a domain
requires *crossing causal support*, not merely overlapping extensional
classification. Point-Strand incidence and Theory can carry many overlaps which
should not be promoted to locality.

Questions:

- which laws truly require a tree rather than only a finite join-semilattice?
- what exactly is the semantic content of parenthood beyond common support/LCA?
- can several independent locality systems coexist externally without becoming
  kernel membranes?
- can a minimal irreducible example force non-laminar causal permission?

The semilattice generalisation is deliberately treated as a hostile alternative,
not an assumed roadmap.

## Matching as dependent unification plus linear allocation

Experiments suggest a compact reading of the matching judgement:

```text
Point/Membrane context   -- non-injective dependent substitution --> exact context
exact Strand fibre       -- injective allocation -----------------> requirements
```

Local target Points and Membranes behave as positive variables: they may acquire
new equalities but cannot violate asserted equality/ancestry. Imported Points are
rigid. Exact Strand authority remains scarce.

A formal declarative account of this decomposition is a high-priority target,
provided it does not elevate current query-engine data structures into ontology.

## Join as congruence, materialisation and transport

Three existing features should be studied together:

- open Strand equations generate lower-dimensional Point/Membrane congruence;
- frame order selects preferred preservation of existing exact identity while
  later parts act as reusable presentations;
- the returned image transports exact carriers through identification,
  materialisation and SCC contraction.

Questions:

- can the congruence/materialisation part of join be characterised by a universal
  property?
- what algebra does construction-relative image transport satisfy under staged
  joins?
- is frame/template polarity a known substitution/context construction?
- can boundary be characterised as a reflection or support-closing projection?

## Mathematical classification

The raw Face/Strand causal skeleton remains close to occurrence-net,
monogamous-open-hypergraph and string-diagram structures. Point substitution has
neighbours in high-level or identifier-bearing nets; exact Strand multiplicity
has neighbours in individual-token semantics; membrane hierarchy has neighbours
in hierarchical graphs and bigraphical locality.

The complete object now appears richer because ordered Point-Strand incidence is
itself mathematically active, rather than merely payload attached to causal
edges.

Concrete comparison targets:

- open hypergraphs with ordered hyperedge incidence and exact occurrence fibres;
- nominal/dependent substitution systems with fresh-name materialisation;
- linear-resource fibrations over structural/unification contexts;
- cospan/corelation and factor-graph calculi for non-causal Point-Strand scope;
- occurrence/event/HDA notions for commuting causal Developments;
- categorical accounts of frame-biased quotient/materialisation.

The goal is classification after the object settles, not premature identification
with a familiar formalism.

## Semantic compression

A central research hypothesis remains that familiar programming notions such as
ownership-like authority, conflict, concurrency, interface, generic
substitution, lifetime, live state, reusable presentations and freshness are not
independent kernel primitives but derived views of the four-carrier geometry.

The stronger 0.6.2 question is whether important mathematical notions such as
factor scope, hidden-variable elimination and realisation order also separate
naturally along existing incidences rather than requiring new ontology.

This must be attacked rather than celebrated: seek domains where the reduction is
forced, domains where it is merely possible, and domains where it fails.

## Observational equality

Raw exact boundary is future-operational state, but 0.6 does not claim it is the
coarsest observational state. Fresh allocator choices, scheduler presentation,
domain symmetry and Theory equivalence can introduce distinctions an observer may
not care about.

A later semantic layer may define support-sensitive contextual equivalence:

```text
X ~= Y  iff every lawful future observation gives the same result
```

and study full abstraction of concrete boundary/Theory representations against
that equivalence.

## Formalisation

Formalisation remains intentionally deferred until the object feels settled.
Pre-formal discipline should nevertheless be strong:

1. state candidate laws independently of implementation;
2. retain minimal witnesses and counterexamples;
3. distinguish released law, observed regularity and conjecture;
4. record ontology extensions which were considered and then collapsed;
5. prefer adversarial domains to confirmatory examples.

When formalisation begins, the likely bottom-up route is:

1. finite Geometry and membrane forest;
2. declarative context substitution and exact scarce allocation;
3. proof that factorised query enumerates exactly that judgement;
4. declarative join congruence/materialisation/SCC normal form;
5. image-transport and boundary/advance theorems;
6. lawful Theory reindexing/projection interfaces;
7. only then broader categorical or proof-theoretic classifications.

No proof assistant or categorical identification is part of the 0.6.2 release
contract.
