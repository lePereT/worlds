exact Witnesses, residual Frontiers and fresh graft. Prove certification
preservation, scarcity, locality, equivariance and non-generation.

coherent exact families. Characterise derived causality and local conflict

`or_else`, `each` and `together`. Do not add resource or concurrency ontology
unless an irreducible failure earns it.

retain exhaustive finite reference semantics as the oracle.
